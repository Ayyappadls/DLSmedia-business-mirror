DIAGNOSIS

What DLS Sees

Tap any insight to see the full WHAT / WHY / IMPACT / WHAT NEXT reasoning.

01 — CASH IS GETTING TRAPPED
₹80K is tied up in customer receivables
Money already earned, not yet collected.
WHAT₹80,000 of completed sales are sitting with customers as unpaid credit.
WHYThe store extends informal credit to regular customers without a firm collection cycle.
IMPACTThat cash isn't available to cover rent, salaries, or debt repayment this month.
WHAT NEXTReview credit terms and tighten the collection cycle before considering new borrowing.
02 — INVENTORY IS CONSUMING CAPITAL
₹2.5L is tied up in inventory
Roughly 40% of monthly revenue sitting on shelves.
WHAT₹2,50,000 of owner capital is currently held as unsold stock.
WHYPurchase cycles are likely running ahead of actual sell-through, especially on slower-moving lines.
IMPACTThat capital can't be used for repayment, marketing, or expansion.
WHAT NEXTIdentify slow-moving stock and consider a clearance or a leaner reorder cycle.
03 — DEBT IS REDUCING FLEXIBILITY
Monthly debt commitments are pressuring available cash
Repayments compete directly with operating needs.
WHATLoan repayments are a fixed monthly draw on operating profit.
WHYDebt was taken to fund inventory and working capital last year.
IMPACTLess room to absorb a slow month or fund growth without more borrowing.
WHAT NEXTAssess real repayment capacity before taking on any additional debt.
